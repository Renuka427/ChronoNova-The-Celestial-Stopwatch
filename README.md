ChronoNova: The Celestial Stopwatch 🌌⏱️
Welcome to ChronoNova, a celestial time-tracking adventure that blends space and time like never before! Track every second, lap, and moment amidst a breathtaking starry backdrop 🌠. With mesmerizing animations, speech-powered interactions 🗣️, and glowing lap rings 🔵, ChronoNova turns time into a cosmic experience 🚀✨.

🚀 Features
Cosmic Aesthetics: Immerse yourself in a beautiful starfield background with shooting stars 🌠.
Interactive Stopwatch: Track time with a glowing, animated stopwatch that reacts to your actions.
Lap Tracking: Record laps with interactive rings 🔵 and visualize your journey.
Speech Synthesis: Let ChronoNova guide you with voice prompts 🗣️ for a hands-free experience.
Responsive Design: Enjoy smooth performance on all devices, from desktops to mobiles.
Real-time Display: Watch the clock in real-time with precision down to milliseconds.
🌟 How to Use
Start the Stopwatch: Click the Start button to begin tracking your time.
Stop the Stopwatch: Click the Stop button to halt the timer and record your results.
Reset the Stopwatch: Click Reset to start a new session.
Record a Lap: Click the glowing Lap Ring to capture a lap time and track your progress.
Voice Feedback: Listen as ChronoNova provides voice prompts for every action 🗣️.
🎨 Technologies Used
HTML: For building the structure.
CSS: For styling the stopwatch and cosmic effects.
JavaScript: For implementing stopwatch functionality and lap tracking.
SpeechSynthesis API: For adding voice-guided features.
